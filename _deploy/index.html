About the company

Roads, buildings, and renovation — done by our own site team

M/S Al-Mughal Brothers is a construction and development contractor based in Muzaffarabad, Azad Jammu & Kashmir, led by Engr. Kamal Ashraf. The company has completed road and water-crossing works across several link-road projects, alongside residential and commercial building work.
